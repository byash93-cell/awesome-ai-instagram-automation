## Video editing and motion
- [Remotion](https://github.com/remotion-dev/remotion) - Make videos with React code; great for reusable reel templates, animated text and transitions. Free for individuals and small teams.
- [Motion Canvas](https://github.com/motion-canvas/motion-canvas) - Code-driven animated graphics for explainer reels.
- [FFmpeg](https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg) - The Swiss-army knife for cutting, resizing and joining clips; its `xfade` filter has 40+ transitions.
- [MoviePy](https://github.com/Zulko/moviepy) - Edit videos with simple Python scripts.

## Captions and speech-to-text
- [whisper.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp) - Fast, CPU-friendly speech-to-text with word-level timestamps for word-by-word captions.
- [faster-whisper](https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper) - A faster Whisper implementation in Python.

## AI voice
- [Kokoro](https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro) - Small, natural-sounding text-to-speech that runs on CPU.
- [Chatterbox](https://github.com/resemble-ai/chatterbox) - Open-source TTS with expressive delivery and voice cloning.

## AI avatars and lip sync
- [LatentSync](https://github.com/bytedance/LatentSync) - High-quality lip sync for talking videos (needs a GPU; try a free Hugging Face demo).
- [MuseTalk](https://github.com/TMElyralab/MuseTalk) - Real-time lip sync for talking avatars.

## Consistent AI characters
- [InstantID](https://github.com/instantX-research/InstantID) - Keep the same face across new images from one reference photo.
- [PuLID](https://github.com/ToTheBeginning/PuLID) - Identity-preserving image generation.

## Screen recording for tutorials
- [OBS Studio](https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio) - Sharp screen and camera recording.
- [Cap](https://github.com/CapSoftware/Cap) - Open-source Screen Studio alternative with auto-zoom on clicks.

## Trend research
- [PRAW](https://github.com/praw-dev/praw) - Official-API Python client to track rising posts on AI subreddits.
- [Hacker News API](https://github.com/HackerNews/API) - Free official API for spotting AI tools as they take off.

## Website and SEO
- [Astro](https://github.com/withastro/astro) - Fast static sites that score well on Core Web Vitals.
- [Lighthouse](https://github.com/GoogleChrome/lighthouse) - Google's free page speed and SEO audit.
- [IndexNow](https://www.indexnow.org/) - Tell Bing and Yandex about new pages instantly.

## Analytics
- [GoatCounter](https://github.com/arp242/goatcounter) - Privacy-friendly, cookie-free website analytics.
- [Umami](https://github.com/umami-software/umami) - Self-hostable website analytics.

## Stay safe on Instagram
- Post through Meta's official tools (Meta Business Suite or the Instagram Graph API).
- Avoid unofficial login bots (auto-follow, auto-comment, private-API clients); they break Instagram's terms and are a common cause of bans.
- Label realistic AI people with Instagram's "AI info" label.
